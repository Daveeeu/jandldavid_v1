## Project Overview

MotoCosmos is a modern cross-platform mobile application built with Flutter and powered by a high-performance backend developed in Go.

The platform is designed specifically for the motorcycle community, bringing together riders, events, tours, businesses and community features into a single digital ecosystem.

The architecture has been designed from the beginning for high availability, scalability and future growth, making it capable of serving hundreds of thousands of users.
